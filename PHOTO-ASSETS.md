# 3Dream product photographs

All primary catalog photographs use the built-in image generation tool. Final optimized files are in `public/images/studio/`. The complete product-to-photo mapping is `lib/studio-images.json`.

## Shared editing brief

Preserve the source product's physical shape, original spool or refill, label, manufacturer, filament color and material finish. Place it on a seamless red studio floor and background with soft lighting and shadows. Keep clear margins. When a spool has no printed example, add one small twisted vase matching the filament. Retain existing printed examples without adding another. When the original is only a color sample, retain that sample without inventing packaging. Do not add prices, marketing copy or a watermark to the generated raster.

The actual 3Dream logo is applied consistently by `ProductPhoto` in the lower-right corner, including product cards, product pages, cart and favorites. Printed sample pieces are illustrative and are not included with the filament.

Original reference images remain accessible in product galleries. Original PNG sources are retained under `source-assets/`; compressed gallery versions are under `public/images/printers/` and `public/images/portfolio/`.

Catalog identities, prices and specifications are preserved. Image generation does not change inventory or payment behavior.
